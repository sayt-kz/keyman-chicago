FAQ

Questions drivers ask

How fast can you get to me?

Response time starts from 30 minutes depending on your location in the Chicago area and traffic. Call us and we'll give you a real ETA.

Can you make a key if I lost all my keys?

Yes. We can create and program a brand-new key for most vehicles even when no working key is available.

Is it cheaper than the dealership?

Usually, yes — and you don't need to tow your car. We come to you with OEM-grade equipment.

Will you damage my car during a lockout?

No. We use professional tools and techniques designed to open vehicles without damage.

Is there a warranty?

Every key we make is covered by a 12-month warranty.

What info do you need for a quote?

Make, model and year of the vehicle, the type of key (regular, remote, smart/push-to-start), and your location.
